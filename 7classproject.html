A Tribute to Albert Einstein

Introduction

Albert Einstein was one of the greatest and most influential physicists of all time. He is best known for developing the theory of relativity.

Key Characteristics

Einstein was famously known for his brilliant intellect, his curious nature, and his iconic wild hair.

Note: Historical records show he loved playing the violin in his free time.

Famous Quotes

A short quote by him: Imagination is more important than knowledge.

A longer reflection on his philosophy of life:

"The important thing is not to stop questioning. Curiosity has its own reason for existing. One cannot help but be in awe when he contemplates the mysteries of eternity, of life, of the marvelous structure of reality."
